## Can Search Data Nowcast Tourism?

Google Trends indices for "new zealand holiday" correlate at 0.71 with monthly visitor arrivals across 2016 to 2026 — a strong full-sample number, but one that is partly an artefact of COVID. Splitting the sample tells a more honest story: the pre-COVID correlation is 0.63, and the post-recovery correlation settles around 0.54 once the sharpest reopening months are excluded.

The practical value is timing. Stats NZ publishes arrivals with a lag of several weeks, while Google Trends updates within days. Search data can therefore act as one input in a nowcasting model, particularly at turning points.

## AI Adoption on the Ground

The same brief reviews AI use across New Zealand tourism and hospitality. Adoption is accelerating but capability is the bottleneck: small operators report limited staff time and expertise, and only 34 percent of surveyed workers describe themselves as AI-literate.

## What This Means

Search-based nowcasting is useful but not decisive on its own. Privacy thresholds, revision risk, and spurious correlation all need disciplined handling. The sensible role for search data is as one input alongside seasonal, booking, and structural signals.
