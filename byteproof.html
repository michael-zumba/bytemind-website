Academic journal (top-tier)

For manuscripts heading to top-tier journals, ByteProof tightens grammar, punctuation and flow while preserving the argument.

Before

"This paper investigates the relationship between corporate governance and firm performance. We find that firms with stronger governance exhibit significantly better performance, however, this effect is moderated by the level of institutional ownership. Interestingly, the effect is stronger in firms with higher analyst coverage. It is important to note that our results are robust to a number of different specifications, including the inclusion of additional control variables and the exclusion of outliers."

After

"This paper investigates the relationship between corporate governance and firm performance. We find that firms with stronger governance perform significantly better, but this effect is moderated by institutional ownership. The effect is stronger in firms with higher analyst coverage. Our results are robust to alternative specifications, including additional control variables and the exclusion of outliers."

Wordy academic phrases flagged and removed, without changing the findings.

General email

A routine team email: filler is removed, the tone stays friendly, and the message is clear.

Before

"Hi team, just wanted to reach out to let you know that the meeting has been rescheduled, it is now on Thursday at 10am instead of Wednesday. Sorry for any inconvenience. Also, please note that the agenda has been updated and is now available in the shared folder for anyone who wants to take a look at it before the meeting."

After

"Hi team, the meeting has moved to Thursday at 10 am instead of Wednesday. Apologies for the inconvenience. The agenda has also been updated and is available in the shared folder, so you can review it before the meeting."

A wordy email becomes clear, friendly and ready to send.

PhD thesis

A chapter draft: meaning and the candidate's voice are preserved while redundant phrasing is trimmed.

Before

"In order to examine the relationship between corporate governance and firm value, this chapter draws upon agency theory. The analysis focuses on the role of independent directors, and how their presence affects monitoring quality. It is worth noting that the existing literature has largely overlooked the role of director tenure in explaining monitoring effectiveness."

After

"To examine the relationship between corporate governance and firm value, this chapter draws on agency theory. It focuses on the role of independent directors and how their presence affects monitoring quality. Existing research has largely overlooked the role of director tenure in explaining monitoring effectiveness."

Meaning and voice preserved; redundant phrases cut.

General language editing

A professional letter: formal but clear, with outdated filler removed and the message made direct.

Before

"We are writing to inform you that, in accordance with our records, your account currently shows an outstanding balance of $450, which is overdue for payment since the 1st of March. Kindly arrange for the settlement of the amount at your earliest convenience. Should you have any queries regarding this matter, please do not hesitate to contact our accounts team."

After

"We are writing to let you know that your account has an outstanding balance of $450, overdue since 1 March. Please arrange payment at your earliest convenience. If you have any questions, please contact our accounts team."

Formal but direct, with outdated filler removed.

How does ByteProof work? +

Select the text you want proofread, then press the hotkey (Cmd+Shift+' on macOS, Ctrl+Shift+' on Windows). ByteProof reads the selection, processes it with its private Local AI or the provider you chose, and shows the proposed changes in its window.

In Microsoft Word, the changes are applied as tracked changes, with optional comments. In any other app, you get a clean final version to apply or copy.

Which AI models can I use? +

Out of the box, ByteProof uses its private Local AI: a Qwen3 model that runs entirely on your computer. It picks the right size for your RAM — no account, no API key, no subscription.

You can also connect DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, Groq, Perplexity, or your own Ollama server with your keys.

Is my writing private? +

With Local AI, your text is processed entirely on your computer and never leaves it. That is the default.

If you connect a cloud provider, ByteProof only sends the text you select to that provider. API keys are stored in the app on your computer and are not shared.

Does Word integration work on Windows too? +

Yes. ByteProof supports Microsoft Word on both macOS and Windows, including tracked changes and comments. Outside Word, the any-app polish mode works on both platforms.

Do I need Microsoft Word? +

Only for tracked-changes proofreading. Without Word, ByteProof still polishes selected text in email, browsers, editors and other applications.

What are the system requirements? +

macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel Macs, or Windows 10 / 11. Local AI recommends 8 GB RAM or more; the app picks the best model for your computer.

On macOS, grant Accessibility permission when prompted so global hotkeys and Word integration can work.
