## Summary

New Zealand's tax system is broad-based and low-rate, with no general capital gains tax, no stamp duty, no payroll tax, and no tax-free threshold. That structure means legitimate tax optimisation for a small or medium enterprise and its owners is rarely about exotic structures. It comes down to three disciplined activities: choosing the right entity, claiming every deduction the business is genuinely entitled to, and managing timing and compliance to avoid interest and penalties.

## Key Points

**Rate alignment has narrowed the arbitrage.** Since the top personal rate and the trustee rate were aligned at 39 percent from the 2024/25 income year, trusts are no longer an income-tax rate shelter. The 28 percent company rate still sits below the top personal rate, but for a 39 percent shareholder this is a deferral, not a permanent saving: the dividend top-up on distribution restores the full 39 percent burden.

**Deductions are the largest aggregate saving.** For most SMEs the biggest reliable savings come from claiming every legitimate deduction with adequate records, including the correct home-office proportion, the right vehicle method, the 50 percent entertainment split, and the Budget 2025 Investment Boost (a 20 percent upfront deduction on eligible new assets first available for use on or after 22 May 2025).

**The provisional tax safe harbour is valuable and under-used.** Where prior-year residual income tax is under $60,000, paying the standard uplift on time protects against use-of-money interest, which runs at 8.97 percent from 16 January 2026.

**Use the incentives Parliament provided.** The 28 percent PIR cap on PIE income, the KiwiSaver government contribution, and the 15 percent R&D Tax Incentive are legitimate, deliberate reliefs that many owners leave on the table.

## The Boundary

Every lever in the brief is legitimate only where it reflects genuine commercial and personal reality. The general anti-avoidance rule (sections BG 1 and GA 1 of the Income Tax Act 2007) lets the Commissioner void arrangements whose dominant purpose is to defeat the intent of the Act. The safe path is substance: genuine services for salaries, genuine shareholdings for dividends, and genuine work for family remuneration.
