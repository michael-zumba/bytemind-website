## Third-party source data

The source listings, descriptions, photographs and some valuation data originate
from Trade Me and Homes.co.nz. Their terms and copyright remain with their
owners. This release deliberately excludes raw descriptions, photos, agent
contact details, member IDs and source URLs. Anyone reusing the derived data is
responsible for complying with the source sites' terms and applicable law.

## No advice

This is research data, not a valuation, appraisal or financial advice. Model
outputs are estimates with documented error and should be read with the
accompanying manifest and methodology.
