## From White Paper to Mainstream

Bitcoin's first block was mined in January 2009. Seventeen years later, the total cryptocurrency market capitalisation reached a peak of USD 4.31 trillion in October 2025, and institutional adoption has moved far beyond the early enthusiast phase.

Corporate treasury exposure is now significant. Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) holds 843,775 Bitcoin, the largest corporate holding by a wide margin, while US spot Bitcoin ETFs have accumulated more than USD 51 billion in net inflows since January 2024.

## Corporate Adoption and Regulation

The accounting picture changed decisively with FASB ASU 2023-08, which replaced the impairment-only cost model with fair value measurement. The EU's MiCA regulation, fully applicable from December 2024, provides the first comprehensive cross-border framework for crypto-assets, while 146 countries are exploring central bank digital currencies.

Not everything has succeeded. The IBM-Maersk TradeLens consortium was discontinued in 2022 after failing to reach commercial viability, a reminder that blockchain adoption depends on coordination across an entire network, not just technical capability.

## What to Watch

The 2022 failures of Terra/LUNA, FTX, and several crypto lenders destroyed over USD 100 billion in market value. The lesson the report draws is that those were failures of centralised intermediaries, not of the underlying blockchain protocols — a distinction that matters for both investors and researchers.
