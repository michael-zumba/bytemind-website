Everyone has an opinion about NZ house prices. But what does the data actually show?

I spent the past week testing the common assumptions against official numbers from Stats NZ, the Reserve Bank, REINZ, and CoreLogic. A few things surprised me.

Overseas buyers were never a big factor nationally. At their peak in 2018, they made up just 3.3% of home transfers. After the foreign buyer ban, that fell to 0.4%. House prices then rose 65% with overseas buyers out of the picture.

First-home buyers are not being squeezed out. They reached a record 27.5% of all purchases in 2025. About 24,800 properties bought by first-home buyers in the past twelve months.

The market is deeply split. Auckland and Wellington are still 22-26% below their 2021 peaks. Canterbury and Southland have hit new records.

More than half of first-home buyer loans are now low-deposit. A record 51.8% had less than 20% equity in January 2026.
