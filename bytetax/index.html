Before you trust it

Every answer shows its working

Tax changes. A learning tool that quietly teaches last year's rule is worse than no tool at all, so accuracy is the first design decision here, not a feature added later.

Traced to the source

Each question rests on at least one Inland Revenue page, and the exact words that prove it are stored with it.

Checked by machine

A validator looks for those words in the page it cites. If the wording moves, the question fails and is held back until someone re-reads it.

Rates carry dates

A number is never stored bare. Rates keep the period they apply to, so a 2024 rate cannot be served as today's.

Backed by the statute

Key rules cite the Income Tax Act 2007, the GST Act 1985 or the Tax Administration Act 1994 alongside the Inland Revenue explanation.

Plain about what it is

General information, not tax advice, and it says so on every screen. Inland Revenue remains the authority.

Nothing leaves your device

Progress is kept in your own browser, or on your own machine in the desktop app. No account, no tracking.

Content checked against Inland Revenue on 21 September 2026. 120 facts from 60 sources behind 200 questions.
