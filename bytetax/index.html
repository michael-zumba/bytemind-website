Before you trust it

Every answer shows where it came from

Tax rules change. A game that quietly teaches an old rule is worse than no game at all. So accuracy came first here.

Every answer has a source

Each question rests on at least one Inland Revenue page. The exact words that prove it are stored with it.

A computer checks the words

If the page changes and the wording moves, the question fails and is held back until a person reads it again.

Numbers carry a date

A rate is never stored on its own. It keeps the years it applied to, so a 2024 rate cannot be served as today's.

Backed by the law

Key rules also cite the Income Tax Act 2007, the GST Act 1985 or the Tax Administration Act 1994.

Clear about what it is

General information, not tax advice. It says so on every screen, and Inland Revenue stays the authority.

Nothing leaves your device

Your progress is kept in your own browser. No account, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere.
