## How LLMs Work

At their core, large language models predict the next word fragment in a sequence. The transformer architecture lets them process an entire context in parallel, which is what made training at massive scale feasible. Training compute has grown roughly 4.5-fold per year since 2010, and frontier models are now estimated in the trillions of parameters.

## What They Can and Cannot Do

LLMs are excellent pattern recognisers but have no built-in truth-checking mechanism. Hallucination rates have improved on document summarisation benchmarks — the best mid-2026 models are around 1 to 3 percent — but open-ended generation remains riskier. The rule for academic users is simple: verify every factual claim against a primary source.

## Why Accounting Researchers Should Care

LLMs are already reshaping research workflows, publishing policy, and professional practice. Publishers prohibit listing AI as an author but allow disclosed AI-assisted writing. Meanwhile, firms are integrating GenAI into audit and advisory work, creating natural experiments for research on disclosure, audit quality, and governance.
