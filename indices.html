Housing Market Indicators

Long-run property market data for New Zealand and Australia, sourced from the Bank for International Settlements, OECD, Reserve Bank of New Zealand, and Stats NZ.

About the Data ▼

Real House Price Indices

Harmonised long series from the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), base year 2010=100, deflated by CPI. Available from 1970 for both New Zealand and Australia. These are the most credible cross-country comparable house price series available.

Source: BIS Residential Property Price Statistics, Q3 2025 release.

Price-to-Income Ratio

OECD Affordable Housing Database indicator, seasonally adjusted, 2015=100. Tracks the relationship between nominal house prices and nominal disposable income per capita. The long-run median (1986-2025) is 88.9.

Source: OECD Affordable Housing Database, Indicator HM1.2.

Real HPI Deviation from Trend

Percentage deviation of the real HPI from its long-run stochastic trend, estimated using a one-sided Hodrick-Prescott filter (lambda=400,000). Positive values indicate overvaluation relative to trend.

Mortgage Rates & Household DTI

1-year fixed mortgage rates from RBNZ B20 series. Household debt-to-disposable-income ratio from RBNZ key statistics.

Source: RBNZ B20, C40, and household debt statistics.

Building Consents & Net Migration

Annual building consents (dwellings, thousands) and net international migration (thousands) from Stats NZ/Tatauranga Aotearoa.

Source: Stats NZ Building Consents Issued and International Migration releases.
