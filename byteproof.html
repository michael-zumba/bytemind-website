How it works

Select. Review. Apply.

Three steps, one hotkey. Each clip below is the real app, about three seconds long.

1

Select the text, press the hotkey

Cmd+Shift+' on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+' on Windows. ByteProof reads the words around your selection too.

2

Review every change

Each suggestion shows the original, the replacement, and a short reason. Apply the ones you want, or dismiss the rest.

3

Apply all — or undo

Apply every change in one click. If you change your mind, Undo puts the original text back.

Before and after

See the difference.

Four kinds of writing. ByteProof keeps your meaning and cuts what gets in the way.

Academic journal

For a manuscript heading to a top-tier journal: wordy phrasing trimmed, argument untouched.

Before

"We utilise a range of methods in order to examine the relationship due to the fact that the data is limited."

After

"We use a range of methods to examine the relationship because the data is limited."

Three phrases cut. The finding is unchanged.

General email

A routine team email: filler removed, tone kept friendly.

Before

"Hi team, the meeting has been rescheduled to Thursday at 10am instead of Wednesday. Sorry for any inconvenience. The agenda has been updated and is in the shared folder if you want to look before the meeting."

After

"Hi team, the meeting has moved to Thursday at 10 am instead of Wednesday. Apologies for the inconvenience. The agenda is updated and in the shared folder, so you can review it beforehand."

Shorter, still friendly, ready to send.

PhD thesis

A chapter draft: your voice and your argument stay, repetition goes.

Before

"To examine the relationship between corporate governance and firm value, this chapter draws on agency theory. The analysis focuses on the role of independent directors and how their presence affects monitoring quality."

After

"To examine the relationship between corporate governance and firm value, this chapter draws on agency theory. It focuses on independent directors and how their presence affects monitoring quality."

Meaning and voice kept; the repeated clause removed.

General language editing

A professional letter: formal, and clear about what you want.

Before

"Your account shows an outstanding balance of $450, overdue since 1 March. Please pay as soon as you can. If you have any questions, contact our accounts team."

After

"Your account has an outstanding balance of $450, overdue since 1 March. Please arrange payment at your earliest convenience. If you have any questions, please contact our accounts team."

Formal, direct, no filler.

How does ByteProof work? +

Select the text, then press the hotkey — Cmd+Shift+' on macOS, Ctrl+Shift+' on Windows. The suggestions appear in ByteProof's panel.

In Word they become tracked changes. Anywhere else, the selected text is replaced with the polished version.

Which AI models can I use? +

ByteProof's own local model by default: a Qwen3 model that runs on your computer, sized to suit your RAM. No account, no API key.

You can also connect DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, Groq, Perplexity or Ollama.

Is my writing private? +

With local AI, yes. Your text is processed on your computer and never leaves it.

If you connect a cloud provider, only the text you select is sent, using your own key.

Do I need Microsoft Word, and does it work on Windows? +

Word is only needed for tracked changes, and it works on macOS and Windows, comments included.

Without Word, ByteProof still polishes text you select in email, browsers, editors and other applications.

What are the system requirements? +

macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel, or Windows 10 / 11. Local AI recommends 8 GB RAM or more; the app picks the model for your machine.

On macOS, grant Accessibility permission when asked, so the hotkey and Word integration can work.

How much does it cost? +

7 days free with every feature, then a limited free mode (local AI, 3 proofreads a day) if you do nothing.

A licence is a one-time NZ$49, GST included, and unlocks everything. No subscription.

How do I activate my license? +

Polar emails your key after checkout. Open ByteProof → Settings → License → "Already Paid? Activate with License Key", and paste it in.

One licence covers 2 computers. To move machines, choose "Deactivate This Computer" on the old one first.

macOS says it can't verify ByteProof — is it safe? +

Yes. ByteProof is built and signed by ByteMind Ltd; the warning appears while Apple's notarization completes.

To open it the first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway next to ByteProof, then confirm.
